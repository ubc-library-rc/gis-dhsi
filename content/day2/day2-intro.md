# Day 2: Mapping with QGIS

Day 2 will provide a conceptual and practical orientation to geographic information systems (GIS), a software often used to analyze, modify, and visualize spatial data. You will develop familiarity with QGIS — a free and open-source GIS — by solving puzzles involving common tools and workflows. Finally, you will gain practical experience making maps of your own with QGIS.
