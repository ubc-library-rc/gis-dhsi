# Hands On with QGIS
Outline what we will do now and in afternoon. 

The rest of the morning session will be a practical introduction to QGIS. We will explore the QGIS interface and historical data for Montreal. Then, we'll make an example reference map to put into practice what we've learned. 

When we return in the afternoon, you'll be introduced to common tools and workflows in QGIS, many aimed at making thematic maps. You'll have the chance to solve spatial puzzles to practice computational thinking. Then, you'll create a choropleth and proportional symbol map based on 1881 Census Data. 

Why reference and thematic mapping are relevant to digital humanities....
