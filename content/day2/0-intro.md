# Day 2: Mapping with QGIS

Today's lesson will provide a conceptual and practical orientation to geographic information systems (GIS), a software used to analyze, modify, and visualize spatial data. You will develop familiarity with [QGIS](https://qgis.org/){:target="_blank"} — a free and open-source GIS — by creating reference maps and thematic maps with two sets of data. 

In the morning, you'll make a reference map of Montréal's historic public bath houses. In the afternoon, we'll work with pre-prepared historic data from the public history project *[Montréal, l’avenir du passé (MAP)]([Montréal, l’avenir du passé (MAP)](https://mun.ca/mapm/fra/accueil_cadre.html)){:target="_blank"}* (*Montréal, The Future of the Past*) to create thematic maps visualizing demographic data from the 1880s. Tomorrow we'll return to the data used this morning as we explore common tools and workflows in QGIS.
