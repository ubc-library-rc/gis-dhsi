# Spatial Visualization and Digital Humanities

Let’s take a look at a few example maps to think about different map/spatial visualization types.

As we are looking at these maps, consider the following questions:
- What are the components of the map?
- What features does it have?
- How do you interact with it?
- What argument does the map make?
- How does it communicate its argument?
