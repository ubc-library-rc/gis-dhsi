# Day 4: Narrative Maps

Today we will learn to georeference historical maps and build multi-media narratives. Multi-media narratives combine text, maps, videos, timelines, and more to convey spatial information in the form of an interactive story. 

 <!-- You will learn how to georeference digitized maps using IIIF and QGIS, and explore the capabilities of both ArcGIS StoryMaps (the free version) and Knightlab StoryMaps, a free and open-source alternative to ArcGIS. As with all other tools introduced throughout our course, we will discuss the relative advantages and disadvantages of each platform, and show you how to get started using them.  -->

## Lesson Plan 
**Morning Session**
- Georeferencing digitized maps using IIIF and QGIS

**Afternoon Session** 
- Creating StoryMaps using ArcGIS StoryMaps (the free version) and Knightlab StoryMaps, a free and open-source alternative to ArcGIS.
